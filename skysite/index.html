welcome to
skyshot online

hai :3
my name is skylar, 24 years old, and trans woman extraordinare. this website acts as an extension of the self within the online cyber world. this is the main product of a half-degree in computer science and a hatred for what the majority of the modern internet has become. i love screamo and electronic dance music, JRPGs, shoujo manga, networking (computers), making webs (also computers), and fucking (still computers). I occasionally draw, write poetry, and dj.

as someone who spent a nearly a decade on tumblr in the 2010s, a major part of my enjoyment stemmed from customizing your blog. stripping and stealing scripts and themes from other blogs to put onto my own, tweaking each thing, learning HTML and CSS from that, was all very pivotal for me. the major social media sites in the 2020s have completely stripped all of those personal touches from a given persons social media presence. all you get is a profile page with an icon, maybe a banner, and your posts. i've seen profiles where users will try to cram as much of their aesthetic into their bio, when if you could pick up the website fundamentals, could create something much more imaginative, vast, expressive, and representative. this site is closer to defining my tastes than what you could get out of one of my feeds. i hope you enjoy the experience of seeing and going through everything ive handwritten and debugged on my computer <3

Look to the sea above and find guidance and grace to find me across the net.
